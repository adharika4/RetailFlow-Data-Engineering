About this analysis

The ETL pipeline excludes cancelled invoices, invalid quantities/prices and POST postage entries, then loads validated positive sales lines into a SQLite star schema. Unknown customer IDs are preserved. Some other service or adjustment stock codes may remain (for example DOTCOM POSTAGE). Reported revenue is not net revenue after refunds. Data: UCI Online Retail (Chen, 2015), CC BY 4.0.
